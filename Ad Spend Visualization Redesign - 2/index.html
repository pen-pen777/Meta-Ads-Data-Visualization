Method

Source: Meta Ad Library , US social issue, election and politics ad disclosures. Aggregate file: 14,071 advertisers deduplicated by Page ID, last 30 days. Per-ad detail covers 782, 263 and 72 ads for Ground News, Democrats and Abdul El-Sayed respectively. All figures are estimates taken as midpoints of Meta's disclosed ranges, not exact amounts. State boundaries: Natural Earth via us-atlas.

Reading the file

Two spend figures appear throughout. Aggregate spend comes from Meta's advertiser-level file and drives the leaderboard. Per-ad spend is the sum of the individual ad disclosures and drives every reach and efficiency figure, because only those ads carry impression ranges.
